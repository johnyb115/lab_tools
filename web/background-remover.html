🪄 Background Remover

Cuts the background out of a photo entirely in your browser using a small AI segmentation model, and gives you back a transparent PNG. Nothing is uploaded to a server. The first run downloads the model (roughly 5–15s depending on your connection); it's cached after that, so repeat visits are fast.
