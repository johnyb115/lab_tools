📝 Word → High-Quality PDF

Convert a .docx to PDF without Word's own "Save as PDF" recompressing your figures. Everything runs locally in your browser — the file never leaves your machine.

Why this helps: Word's PDF exporter downsamples embedded images to a fixed print resolution on the way out, even if the originals are much sharper. This tool instead reads the original image bytes straight out of the .docx and hands them to your browser's own print engine, which does not recompress them — so the PDF you save keeps the original image quality. It's optimized for text + image fidelity, not exact Word layout: complex tables, headers/footers and exact page breaks may not match Word pixel-for-pixel.
