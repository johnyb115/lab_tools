🧪 Lab Tools

A single home for the small research utilities the group used to run as separate Streamlit apps. Everything below runs entirely in your browser — files you upload are never sent anywhere.

⚡
Voltammetry (CV/DPV) Plotter

Upload Autolab/NOVA CV or DPV exports, pick a scan range, and get an interactive combined plot. Batch-export processed CSVs as a ZIP.

CSV / TXT 📈
Universal Data Plotter

Load any CSV, ASCII or XLSX table, choose X/Y columns, toggle individual traces on and off, and customize axes, colors and scales.

CSV / ASCII / XLSX 🖼️
Plot Digitizer

Turn a graph screenshot back into numbers. Calibrate the axes, click on a curve's color, and extract its data points to CSV.

PNG / JPG 🧮
Four-Point Probe

Fit I–V sweeps, compute sheet resistance, and apply the Ossila rectangular-geometry correction. Batch multiple runs of one sample.

ASC / CSV / TXT 🔢
Linspace Generator

Generate evenly spaced numbers between a min and max and download them as CSV.

Utility ✂️
Image Auto-Crop

Automatically trim uniform white-space margins around an uploaded image.

PNG / JPG 🪄
Background Remover

Cut the background out of a photo entirely in-browser and download a transparent PNG.

PNG / JPG 📝
Word → High-Quality PDF

Convert a .docx to PDF without Word's default image recompression — figures keep their original resolution.

DOCX 🌀
EIS Plotter

Load impedance spectroscopy data and get Nyquist and Bode plots, with per-file column mapping and overlay of multiple runs.

CSV / ASCII / XLSX ⛰️
Peak Integrator

Load a curve, click two baseline points, and get peak height and integrated area (e.g. for CV/DPV peaks or chromatography traces).

CSV / ASCII / XLSX 📤
PDF Image Extractor

Pull the embedded figures back out of a PDF at their native pixel resolution as individual PNGs.

PDF 📏
Scale Bar Calibration

Click two points a known distance apart on a micrograph or photo and burn a calibrated scale bar into a copy of the image.

PNG / JPG 🔁
Table Format Converter

Convert between CSV, TSV, XLSX and JSON tables, with a preview before you download.

CSV / TSV / XLSX / JSON 🎯
Uncertainty & Stats

Mean, SD, SEM and confidence intervals for one dataset, or a two-sample t-test to compare two.

Utility 🔲
PCB QR Generator

Generate QR codes with decorative PCB-style traces and vias. Customize colors, geometry, and download as layered SVG.

SVG
