🖼️ Plot Digitizer

Upload a screenshot or photo of a graph, calibrate the X and Y axes by clicking two known points on each, then sample a curve's color to extract its underlying (x, y) data as CSV. Works for any axis range and any number of curves — everything runs locally in your browser, nothing is uploaded anywhere.
